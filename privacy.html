gog-mail Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-07

What gog-mail is

gog-mail is a personal, single-user tool. It is used only by its owner, only with the owner's own Google accounts, and only on the owner's private server. It is not a public service.

Data accessed

When the owner authorizes the tool with Google OAuth, it may access Gmail data of the owner's own accounts (message headers and content, labels) in order to search, summarize, label and archive mail.

How data is used

Data is used only to perform the mailbox organization and reporting the owner requests. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Storage and sharing

  • OAuth tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's private server and are not shared with anyone.
  • Mail data is processed on the owner's server. Short excerpts may be sent to an AI model chosen by the owner solely to produce summaries and reports for the owner.
  • Data is not sold, rented or transferred to third parties, and no human other than the owner reads it.

Google API Services User Data Policy

gog-mail's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and revocation

The owner can revoke the tool's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and delete stored tokens from the server.

Contact

Use the support email shown on the Google consent screen.
